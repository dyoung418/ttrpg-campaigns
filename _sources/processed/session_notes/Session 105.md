*** Echoes in Nerissa’s House: Ghosts, Traps, and the Shadow Hall
** Session Overview
The session picked up in the middle of a fight with the ghostly guards in the courtyard gatehouse area and then expanded into a long, eerie exploration of Nerissa’s house. The party spent the early part of the session finishing off the ghost encounter through a mix of forgiveness-themed roleplay, healing, ranged pressure, and careful positioning. Once the last ghost dissipated, the group paused to notice that the entire house seemed warped by temporal distortions: walls looked ancient in one place and freshly built in another, chalk marks changed mid-drawing, and the courtyard itself seemed to hold echoes of things that had happened or might yet happen.

From there, the party moved through connected rooms and corridors, discovering a meditation chamber with a nonfunctional teleportation circle, a storeroom with preserved food that seemed to shift with time, a salon that turned out to be a telepathic trap or haunting, and a domed room with a taxidermy black bear that animated into a grotesque dance and then exploded. After a healing and recovery break, the group continued into a lower hall painted with a twilight forest where a shadow creature emerged from a painting, split into or used shadow doubles, and launched a brutal area attack. The session ended with one shadow foe heavily damaged, one of the party’s melee responders trying to restrain the caster, and the combat still unresolved as the group prepared to continue next time.

A major roleplay thread running through the whole session was the party’s ongoing conversation about Briara and what Nerissa wants from the sword. The group questioned Briara directly about her desires, her preferred form, and whether she wants reunion with her lesser half. Briara made it clear she wanted Nerissa to beg to be reunited, while the party remained deeply suspicious that the sword might be a conduit,
a trap, or a vessel for Nerissa’s awareness.

** Detailed Story Events
Courtyard gatehouse battle area: The party resumed combat against the ghostly guards right where they had left off. Danny opened by stating, “we’re resuming our battle with the ghostly guards and it’s Pavel’s turn.” Pavel held up Briara and pleaded for her help, framing the enemies as spirits in need of forgiveness: “Sword Briara, we really need your help here. These ghosts are in dire need of forgiveness.” A critical success followed, and one ghost heard a voice that rang true enough to Nerissa’s voice that its eyes filled with tears before it dissipated. Briara responded emotionally afterward, saying, “I’m going to have some words with Nerissa at the end of this. Why do I have to do all the work?” Lucas was then healed by Pavel for 43 healing, arriving “just in time for the ghostly guard… to bear down.” The remaining ghosts pressed the assault, including rapier strikes, an ectoplasmic maneuver that disarmed Briara from Pavel, and a series of attacks and positional adjustments while the party continued trading blows and forgiveness attempts.

Courtyard gatehouse battle area: The rest of the ghost fight unfolded as a long, tense exchange of attacks, healing, and tactical movement. Riven, high above and far away from the earlier forgiveness conversation, shot one of the ghostly guards with a hunted shot while descending toward the ground, and that ghost dissipated right before his eyes. Lucas’s turn included healing and then attempting to forgive one of the remaining ghosts “in the name of Isis,” but the check failed. The party talked through battle medicine immunity, positioning, and who was hurt most badly; Lucas used Battle Medicine and later healing on Lee and Astor, while Astor and others coordinated movement to keep the line from collapsing. The last ghost eventually went down after another critical success on forgiveness, and Danny ended the encounter with, “that takes
us out of combat.”

Courtyard of Nerissa’s house: With combat over, the party immediately began discussing the disturbing nature of the place. Danny explained that “things seem strange” across the house: some walls looked “a thousand years” old while others looked brand new, and there were areas where the environment seemed to contain the imprint of things that had already happened. Astor pointed out that a gouge in the wall matched the mark his weapon had made only moments before, as if the house had already recorded the event. Lucas connected the phenomenon to other time-warped places they had seen, referencing that time under Candlemere had turned out to be months instead of days, and suggesting that time here might flow backward or in loops. The group used this moment to rest, heal, and try to make sense of the anomaly before moving deeper.

Courtyard near the blue tree: Riven noticed a tree with faint glow under the bark and brought it to the group’s attention. Lucas tested the courtyard with chalk, marking surfaces and watching to see if the marks would fade or shift. They did: he drew on a wall and noticed that half of the mark stayed bright while the rest continued in a faded state “where your hand was about to go.” Danny explained that the blue tree was in the courtyard and that the whole area showed strange time-dilation effects, with some plants looking like spring and others like autumn. The party had not yet found something overtly hostile here, but they clearly recognized the place as unstable and unusual.

Courtyard outside Nerissa’s house: The party turned to Briara and Nerissa directly. They discussed whether Nerissa might be watching through the sword and questioned Briara’s desires, her preferred form, and whether she wanted to reunite with her lesser half. Briara’s answer was sharp and emotional: she wanted her other half to “beg to be reunited.” The conversation also established that Briara had been flirting with some party
members but not others, and that with Milo she had seemed almost maternal. This scene deepened the sense that the sword had its own personality, preferences, and private agenda, and that the party was still trying to determine whether it should be trusted.

Courtyard exterior near stable and tower door: The party shifted to exploration and checked the doors leading off the courtyard. Riven listened at one and identified it as a stable by smell; the other door led to a tower staircase with a spiral stair going upward. When they listened carefully, they heard crying from inside. The group chose the door with the crying and entered a room with strange decaying plants, an empty pool, and countless small demon statuettes arranged on shelves. The sobbing sound came from those statues, not from a living creature, which immediately made the room feel like another kind of eerie puzzle rather than a straightforward chamber.

Statue room off the courtyard: Inside the statue room, the party noticed that one demon statuette was laughing instead of crying. Pavel pointed it out to Lucas and specifically did not pick it up, clearly wary of a trap or trigger. The group discussed whether changing anything in the room would cause a reaction. Danny explained that a singular statue that differs from the others is often a sign that moving it may make something happen. They also heard confusing sounds nearby and continued probing the room carefully rather than taking the obvious bait.

Corridor beside the courtyard rooms: Pavel picked a lock and the party entered a long corridor lined with many doors. They checked for sounds and traps, moved into a meditation chamber with a stained glass window and tree-shaped green candlesticks, and found a stone dais that turned out to be a teleportation circle. Riven cast Detect Magic and discovered a “distinct lack of magic,” confirming that the circle was present but not functional. Milo later explained that restoring it
would take nearly as much work as creating one from scratch because the runes had faded through “centuries of disuse.” The party also found a damp storeroom with preserved food, dusty shelves, and more windows looking onto the courtyard. Lucas noticed the temporal weirdness again and used chalk here as well, confirming the house’s inconsistent age and decay.

Salon off the corridor: The party opened a salon decorated with frescoes of primeval forests, floral blooms, and beautiful women. Plush sofas and floral arrangements made it feel more refined than the rest of the house, but the room quickly turned uncanny when a telepathic whispering effect began intruding into their minds. Initiative was rolled after the whispers became mentally invasive and difficult to distinguish from the party’s own voices. Riven discovered a magic flute in the room, and the party identified the encounter as a trap or haunting rather than a conventional enemy fight. They ultimately left the room with the flute in hand.

Domed room with caged taxidermy bear: The party moved into a room dominated by a caged stuffed black bear. Lucas played music for it and tried to use magic to dispel the effect, including a 6th level dispel magic cast through the Rod of Negation. The bear completed a dance, and when the third pirouette finished, it exploded. Danny described the room as becoming charred by the blast. The explosion injured multiple party members, and the group had to stop and recover. Astor used Energy Interceptor to try to take Pavel’s damage for him. The party joked grimly about the absurdity of a dead stuffed bear exploding, but the impact was real, and Lucas had to acknowledge that his counteract attempt was not strong enough to stop the magical effect.
Lower hall with painted forest walls: After the explosion and another round of healing, the party pressed forward into a lower hall with a polished marble floor, a carving of the sun being devoured by a great dragon, and wall paintings of a twilight forest with dark shapes among the trees. Pavel detected that something was wrong with the paintings and warned the others not to get close. The group debated whether to cross the room or search for another way, and Riven decided to shoot one of the paintings rather than walk blindly into the suspicious area. As soon as he did, darkness peeled off the painted scene and revealed a shadow creature. Initiative was rolled and combat began. The party learned that these creatures were familiar to Pavel from prior experience and that they served powerful fey or even the eldest beings of the First World.

Lower hall with painted forest walls: The battle against the shadow creature escalated quickly. The enemy flew over the party, used shadowy tactics to create or position shadow doubles, and cast a devastating line effect through the hallway. Lucas struck the creature with a 6th rank Holy Light, hit it with 43 fire damage, and then used Whispers of Weakness to determine that its lowest saving throw was Fortitude and that it had weaknesses, specifically the claws that “look like cold iron” and are weak to it. The enemy’s line attack dealt 104 void damage on failure and 34 additional damage on the return, with Astor noting that if it reduced a creature to zero hit points, that creature died instantly. One of the shadow creatures or doubles was left with only a sliver of life, while others were heavily damaged or destroyed. Astor tried to restrain the caster with a grapple, and Milo/Rindle coordinated with Overdrive, Lock On, pneumatic hammer strikes, and hammer throws. Lee spent part of the turn healing Lucas and preparing to attack. The encounter was still active when the session ended,
with the party bracing for the next round.

** Character Spotlights
* Pavel: Pavel was the emotional center of the forgiveness theme throughout the session. He repeatedly held Briara up and pleaded with ghosts to be forgiven, treating the sword as a partner in mercy rather than a weapon. He also spent a great deal of the session handling doors, checking for traps, opening and closing passages, and generally acting as the party’s cautious lead. His roleplay with Briara was especially strong: he asked what form she preferred, whether she wanted to be reunited with her lesser half, and what he should do with her in the current situation. He also visibly struggled when Briara’s preferences turned toward an elven curve blade and later tried to balance concern for the sword with the party’s growing suspicion.

* Lucas: Lucas handled a wide range of support actions and investigative work. He healed heavily wounded allies, used Battle Medicine, cast Heal spells, and later used Holy Light and Whispers of Weakness against the shadow creature. He also used chalk to test the courtyard’s time distortion and discovered the strange phenomenon that his mark changed partway through drawing. In roleplay, he repeatedly tried to frame Briara’s situation in careful, diplomatic, sometimes doublespeak-heavy language, clearly trying not to offend a sword that might be listening. He also used the Rod of Negation in an attempt to suppress the bear’s magic, and while it failed, the action showed how willing he was to try the strange tools available.

* Riven: Riven was the most suspicious and tactically observant party member this session. He noticed the glowing tree bark, investigated the courtyard for signs of danger, heard the crying in the hallways, and repeatedly pushed the group toward caution. He also acted in combat with ranged pressure, hunting shots, lightning, and coordinated commands for Milo/Rindle. Roleplay-wise, he was deeply skeptical of Briara
and the possibility that Nerissa might be listening through the sword, and he repeatedly checked in with Lucas about what Nerissa might actually want. He was also the one who explicitly asked Briara what she wanted from the encounter, leading directly to her answer that she wanted Nerissa to beg to be reunited.

* Astor: Astor repeatedly connected the strange house to larger metaphysical ideas. He was the first to articulate that the courtyard seemed to record events before or after they happened, and he pressed the idea that the house might contain forewarnings of the future. He also used healing support, first aid, and combat intervention, including stepping in front of blast damage with Energy Interceptor and grappling the shadow caster to keep it from unleashing another massacre-style attack. His roleplay was thoughtful and analytical, especially when he tried to understand the significance of the teleportation circle, the temporal anomalies, and the bizarre room effects.

* Lee: Lee took part in the long ghost battle, helped with healing, and later joined the shadow-fight support and cleanup. He also contributed to the table’s ongoing joking tone, including remarks about the bear explosion and the line about “the perfect finisher.” He was involved in the healing economy, checking who had received battle medicine and who was still hurting, and he also tried to forgive the enemies in the ghost fight before finally ending the combat posture with parry and movement decisions.

* Milo / Rindle: Milo’s actions were notable for the tight mechanical coordination with his companion Rindle. He used Overdrive, Lock On, and directed Rindle to strike and throw hammer attacks in the shadow encounter. Rindle also appears in several of these sequences as the construct or ally being commanded, and the party treated him as a friendly allied presence, not an enemy. Milo also handled repairs and support after the bear explosion, and the group discussed his
ability to restore Rindle between major actions.

** NPCs and World Building
Briara: The sword associated with Nerissa. She appears to have her own voice, agency, and emotional preferences. She was amused and irritated by the ghost fight, and later said she wanted Nerissa to beg to be reunited. She also expressed preferences about weapon form, including enthusiasm for being an elven curve blade.

Nerissa: An important off-screen presence associated with the house and the sword. The party strongly suspected she might be watching through Briara. Aunt Sumi’s warning reinforced this suspicion, saying there was “a presence here watching you” and that she felt it especially strongly whenever the sword spoke.

Aunt Sumi: Lucas’s family member or spiritual guide, appearing in his healing-related exchanges. She warned Lucas to be careful, saying, “Be careful, my grandchild. There’s a presence here watching you.” She also commented on the sword’s voice and how it might indicate Nerissa’s awareness.

Ghostly Guards: Undead or spectral defenders in the courtyard gatehouse. They responded to forgiveness attempts, attacks, and movement, and were eventually forgiven and dissipated.

Maestro’s Flute: Recovered from the salon. Lucas identified it as an instrument suited to master bards and royalty, and chose to keep it.

The House of Nerissa: A major location with temporal distortions, changing wall age, preserved and decayed areas existing side by side, and rooms that seemed to echo past or future events.
Teleportation Circle Room: A meditation chamber with a stained glass window, tree-shaped candlesticks, and a nonfunctional teleportation circle whose runes had faded through centuries of disuse.

Salon: A floral, frescoed room that produced telepathic confusion and led to the recovery of the Maestro’s Flute.

Courtyard Blue Tree / Glowing Tree: A tree with glow under the bark, likely tied to the temporal distortion or the broader oddness of the courtyard.

Shadow Creatures: Fey-associated shadowy killers with claws and wing hooks made from cold iron. They were explicitly described as servants of powerful fey creatures or the eldest beings of the First World.

Plot Threads and Mysteries
Nerissa watching through Briara: The party believes Nerissa may be able to hear or see through the sword. Aunt Sumi’s warning and the sword’s own speech reinforced this concern.

Briara’s true desires: Briara wants Nerissa to beg to be reunited, and she also has clear preferences about what form she wants to take. The party is still trying to determine whether this is a clue to her nature or a trap.

Temporal anomalies in the house: The house contains visible and tangible signs of time distortion. Chalk changes mid-stroke, wall damage appears in advance, and different sections of the house seem to exist in different states of age.

Teleportation circle: The room contained a teleportation circle that is presently nonfunctional. The party discussed whether it could be restored, but Milo believed it would require near-total reconstruction.

The blue-glowing tree: The glow beneath the bark may be a clue, a magical effect, or a trigger for future events. It was not resolved in this session.

Telepathic whispers / haunting in the salon: The source and nature of the whispers were not fully explained, though the party treated them as a complex trap or haunting.
Shadow creatures and First World ties: The shadow foes appear linked to powerful fey or First World entities, and the party’s knowledge of them was incomplete but ominous.

DM Notes and Observations
Pacing: The session had a strong exploration rhythm after the opening combat, with a clear sequence of room discoveries and escalating weirdness. The pace slowed meaningfully during the Briara roleplay, then accelerated again as the party moved through puzzle-like rooms into repeated hazards and combat.

** Player Engagement: The table was highly engaged with both mechanics and story logic. Players continuously cross-checked rules, discussed positioning, and debated magical effects, while also staying invested in the emotional thread around Briara and Nerissa. The persistent jokes about bears, forgiveness, and flirtation did not undercut the seriousness of the mystery; instead, they helped keep the session lively.

** Story Flow: The narrative flowed from immediate combat resolution into a deeper mystery about Nerissa’s house, then into increasingly surreal room-by-room challenges. The house felt like a curated gauntlet of memory, illusion, and fey corruption rather than a normal dungeon. The Briara conversation worked well as a thematic bridge between the ghost fight and the later warnings about being watched.
