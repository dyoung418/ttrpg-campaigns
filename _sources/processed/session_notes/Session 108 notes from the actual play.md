# Recap from last week

## Closing out the Wyvern and Wrigging Man battles
The session began with the continued battle against the wyverns. The wyverns themselves were only a minor threat and very quickly looked like this would be a short battle. Their signature danger was their long reach and abilities that usually had about a 30 ft range. The whole party save Lucas moved into the room with them and began taking them out. But before too long, Wormy (The Wriggling Man) showed up again. The party contained the wyverns by immobilizing one and Milo literally taking control of the other one.  So the battle then became primarily about him.

It still had some damage from the last encounter, but he was still deadly. Lee and Pavel worked on taking him out and Milo caused some splash damage via explosions and things he had in his inventory. The rest of the party threw Area of Effect spells at him or finished off the wyverns. Before too long the wyverns went down and soon after Mr Wormy went down as well due to some timely criticals from Lee and Pavel.

## Discovering the portal
We searched him and the room, but found nothing of note. At this point Astor did one of the critical things of the evening. He recalled that he had noticed something unusually in the throne room behind the throne. We decided to go back to investigate. But first, we healed, refocused and readied ourselves.

The party reentered the throne room and Pavel investigated it disappearing from sight. Soon after Riven and Astor also disappeared when they investigated. The rest of the party took the plunge as well.

The party found themselves in another location even more First World like than the Castle at the Edge of Time (sp?). It was a small space outdoors in a kind of garden courtyard and two people were there: Lyriel and Nyrissa.

## Negotiations with Nyrissa
The party was about 60 feet from Nyrissa and Lyriel whom she held captive. The party began a dialogue with Nyrissa and Danny put us into initiative because a lot was going on very quickly.

Lucas and Riven were primarily focused on talking with Nyrissa. Pavel was preparing for action by working spells as steathily as he could. Lee, Astor and Milo watched and positioned themselves.

Crucially at this point, the party was still not agreed on what to do about Briar and Nyrissa.

There were a few threads going on all at once. Riven moved toward Nyrissa but put away his bow. As he approached, he had to make two very difficult saves - one to avoid being blinded by Nyrissa's beauty and one to avoid taking serious damage. Later another person who moved within 30 feet of Nyrissa had to do the same. He focused on asking Nyrissa to let Freehaven alone, releasing Lyriel, and offering help to Nyrissa against the Lantern King.

Lucas also tried to assess Nyrissa's intentions and tried to get Nyrissa to let go her ambition to destroy Freehaven.

Meanwhile Pavel was casting haste and heroism on himself and generally preparing himself for action.

We were at an impass - Nyrissa demanded Briar in return for Lyriel, but the party was unwilling to threaten Freehaven for the deal. Riven moved closer. Pavel continued to prepare.

Several attempts were made to ask her to stop her ambitions or let Lyriel go. But Nyrissa was having none of it although thanks to several of these requests being "Shameless Requests",  Nyrissa also wasn't angered. Still she was getting short on patience and she still had Lyriel and still threatened Freehaven.

There was a tense moment when Riven offered Nyrissa Briar in return for (1) Lyriel (2) Safe passage for all back to Freehaven (3) No more interference of Nyrissa with Freehaven. Importantly, Nyrissa even offered to do a ritual that would bind the two sides to the agreement.

The party was unsure because Briar might empower Nyrissa and perhaps Nyrissa either wouldn't honor the agreement or would just destroy another kingdom instead sending the region into the first world.

Messages were flying from everyone who had message to others of the party. There were two sides of the choice.

Some believed that returning Briar to Nyrissa would give up the leverage that we had and we didn't know how it would end. She might become more powerful and not embrace the loving side of herself and continue the quest to take down Freehaven and the Stolen Lands.

Others believed that Briar was Nyrissa's - not ours. It was part of Nyrissa (and represented the best of her) and felt she deserved to be made whole with it. They felt it was a show of good faith and offered help to Nyrissa with her trouble. And felt that if the imminent danger to Freehaven and Lyriel were alleviated, the party could deal with whatever danger was still posed by Nyrissa or the Lantern King.

Messages flew and it felt like the party might not support giving Briar back. Several of the party prepared for potential combat - spells were subtley cast, arrows were transformed to cold iron.

Nyrissa asked the party to move back.

In the end, the party agreed to the offer.  At this point, Pavel stepped forward and, very uncharacteristically, became the voice of the party:


## Pavel's speech
Pavel looks uncertainly at his companions, as they slowly retreat from the center of the clearing.  Something about this just doesn’t feel right in his gut.

“Pardon me, Queen Nyrissa,” he said slowly.  “I’m not a king or a magister, and I’m not used to negotiation.  But I am the Warden of Nightmarch, which means that the safety of the people of Freehaven is my job.  Plus, Briara’s in my care.”

He pauses, groping for words.  “I want to give you this sword.  I really do.  But it’s not enough to trade the sword for the life of one person.  We want safety for our people, and we want the friendship of the First World for as long as we, the founders of Freehaven, live and are remembered by our people.

“You are immortal; what are a few years to you?  But for us, and those we love, those years are everything.  We want your friendship, not just an exchange of prisoners.”

Pavel holds his breath, trying to read Nyrissa’s icy body language.  She stares at Pavel, then for an even longer time at Lucas and Riven, as if trying to read their minds.  Finally she nods.

## A Crucial Attempt to Persuade
At this point, Lucas made a crucial attempt for the team to seal the deal.

In preparation, we focused all of our buffs on him. Lucas drank the Silver Tongue Mutagen (+3 item bonus), cast guidance on himself (+1 circumstance bonus), and Riven aided Lucas in his diplomacy at the master level (+3 status bonus), and Danny allowed Riven to use Bon Mot with a quip about how she had been foiled by us repeatedly and needed our help (lower the DC against her will by -3).

The rest of the party was unsure - this would immediately come to blows and be very serious for everyone if it didn't work out.

All in, we needed a 52 (I think) and Lucas rolled and came up short, but hero pointed it and ended with a 53 just enough.

Pavel sighs with relief.  Maybe this can work, he thinks incredulously.

“Lady Nyrissa, I’ve spent my life as an outsider, trying to understand people by watching what they say and do.  I’ve watched this group of men for many years; they are my brothers and friends.  But with Briara in my hand, I’ve also had a chance to learn something new.  Briara acts differently with each one of them:

“To Milo, she acts like a mother figure.  To Astor, like a daughter.

“For Lee she is a flirt; for Riven a swooning admirer; and for Lucas a best friend.”

“I don’t think a sword can make those emotions up.  If this sword’s knowledge of love came from you, then your knowledge of love must have been mighty indeed.

“I think the Lantern King changed you when he ripped this sword out of your soul, and I think you can change by getting it back.”

Here Pavel pauses.  He can feel sweat on the back of his neck, from the strain of trying to put his thoughts into words.

“But here’s the thing, Lady,” he says quietly.  “I don’t think you can seize love, or trade for it, or negotiate for it.  True love can only be given, not demanded.  And in the First World, where gifts and symbolism have tremendous importance, the gift must be not only valuable to the recipient, but also appropriate to the giver.”

Pavel takes a deep breath.  “There’s only one person here who has experience with love as deep and profound as what was taken from you.”  He turns slowly, looking at the companions in the back of the room.  He makes eye contact with Riven and, for once, meets his gaze steadily.

“I’m sorry, Riven,” he says quietly, “but it’s not you.  I know you feel very keenly, but your love for Lyriel and Freehaven… and all of us… it’s all mixed up inside you with guilt and obligation and thoughts of yourself.”

He looks away, embarrassed to have criticized Riven.  “Only one person here has experienced all the forms of love,” he continues.  “As a son, a brother, a father, a husband and a friend.  All of us risk our lives for each other, but only one person consistently takes the blows that are meant for someone else.  Only one person left a beloved family behind, to put his life on the line.

“Only one person does all this instinctively, simply because he can’t conceive of doing anything else.”

His gaze shifts to Astor, who stands apart from the other companions.  “Astor, would you come give Briara to Lady Nyrissa?”

Astor started to move forward.  

Nyrissa was so overwhelmed by the prospect of being whole again that she just let go of Lyriel, there was no careful jockeying to see if both sides would give the prize at the same time, Lyriel arrived safely at the other side of the courtyard before the sword was all the way to Nyrissa and she didn't even notice.  Riven ran to meet Lyriel and hold her.

When Astor closed the distance in front of Nyrissa, as a sign of respect, he knelt and lifted Briara above his head to offer it to Nyrissa. Nyrissa started to reach out to take it, but then hesitated, with a concern across her brow, then swallowed and asked Briara "Is it your desire to be reunited with me?". The response wasn't immediate, but it was with conviction, "yes".

Then, Nyrissa gently reached out and slowly put her hand to the hilt. As her hands touched the blade, a pure white glow grew until it was blinding and swallowed the entire room. No one in the party could see Nyrissa or Astor for the brilliance. It lingered and finally started to dissipate, and when the group could start to make out any figures what they saw was Nyrissa lying on her side on the floor with no sword in sight, silently sobbing.


The adventure began with the friendship of these six - Pavel, Astor, Lee, Milo, Lucas and Riven. And now almost twenty years later the friendship of these same six stood against the hatred of Nyrissa and extended that friendship to Nyrissa.

The campaign officially ended there. And as Danny said, the screen faded to black and the credits rolled.
