## Words, Worms, and Walls in the House at the End of Time

### Session Overview

The session opened with a recap and some table housekeeping inside the House at the End of Time, specifically in the throne area where the party was fighting the Wriggling Man. The GM corrected map visibility and initiative order issues before resuming play with Pavel's turn.

What followed was a long, tactical combat centered on the Wriggling Man, a strange, hard-to-kill creature "composed of words" and later described as a mass of worms. The fight featured reverse gravity, ceiling-to-floor movement, multiple spellcast attempts, sustained area effects, forced repositioning, persistent damage, healing, and a dramatic battlefield split created by a wall of force. The party slowly learned the Wriggling Man's weaknesses, especially to area damage and splash damage, but also discovered that it could resist or evade many precision-focused attacks and had its own teleport-like contingency escape.

After the Wriggling Man vanished, the group spent time regrouping on the second floor walkway, patching wounds, discussing its likely regeneration, and debating whether to pursue immediately or rest. They chose to spend 10 minutes healing and preparing. The party then explored farther into the House and entered a huge ornate chamber with dragon-like supports, statues, chairs, tables, and benches. Hidden pets were revealed beneath the tables, and as soon as they were spotted, they flew out to attack. The GM ended the session at the beginning of that new combat, noting it would likely be shorter than the Wriggling Man fight.
