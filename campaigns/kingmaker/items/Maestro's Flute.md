# Maestro's Flute

*Greater maestro's instrument* — a platinum flute, recovered from the Salon of Confusing Sounds (K13) in
the [[House at the Edge of Time]].

## Current Status

**Held, unexamined.** [[Riven]] found it during the salon encounter in
[[Session 105 - The Thing That Cannot Be Forgiven|Session 105]] and the party carried it out. [[Lucas]]
identified it as an instrument fit for **master bards and royalty** and chose to keep it. As of
[[Session 106 - The Thing He Wears|Session 106]] nobody has identified it properly or used it in play.

## How It Was Found

The salon was the most refined room the party had seen in the House — frescoes of primeval forests, floral
blooms, and beautiful women, plush sofas, flower arrangements. Then a **telepathic whispering** began
pushing into the party's minds, close enough to their own inner voices to be hard to separate from them.
Initiative was rolled, but nothing came out to fight: the room resolved as a **trap or haunting** rather
than a creature encounter. Riven turned up the flute while they were working the room, and they left with
it rather than stay and solve the whispers.

*(The salon is K13, the **Eldritch Echoes** complex hazard — DC 42 Will each round or stupefied and
confused. The party left before it did lasting damage, and the flute is its listed treasure.)*

## GM Notes

> [!note] Unfinished business
> The party never disabled or explained the salon's whispers — they took the loot and walked. The room is
> still live if they come back through, and nobody has asked what the flute *does*. A **greater maestro's
> instrument** in the hands of an oracle/bard who has been carrying the party's social work all campaign is
> a real upgrade for [[Lucas]] the moment someone thinks to identify it.
