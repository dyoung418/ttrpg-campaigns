# Brass Telescope

A large brass telescope with an excellent field of view, taken from the Observatory (K7) of the
[[House at the Edge of Time]]. Worth roughly **3,500 gp**; bulky, but it fits in a bag of holding.

## Current Status

**Held.** Looted in [[Session 106 - The Thing He Wears|Session 106]]. Nobody has studied it since.

## What Makes It Strange

The room it stood in **has no window.** The telescope nonetheless shows **ordinary stars from the Material
Plane** — [[Pavel]] confirmed it, and [[Astor]]'s Perception check confirmed the absence of any opening it
could be looking through. The party swept the room for traps, found nothing, judged the instrument
removable, and took it.

This matters because the House sits in [[Thousandbreaths]], inside the First World, at the edge of time.
Whatever the telescope is pointed at, it is not the sky above it.

## GM Notes

> [!note] An unspent hook
> The party has a working, portable **view of the Material Plane's sky from inside a First World
> demiplane** and has not asked a single question about it. Open threads it could serve:
> - **A fixed reference point.** If it always shows the *same* real sky, it's a way to tell how much
>   Material-Plane time has passed — directly relevant to the House's temporal distortions and to
>   [[Candlemere Island|Candlemere]], where days turned out to be months.
> - **A window that looks the other way.** [[Nyrissa]] has watched a hundred kingdoms across worlds. An
>   observatory in her house is not an idle luxury.
> - **Loot value only**, if it turns out to be nothing more — 3,500 gp toward the runes the
>   **Sard's Heart** was supposed to pay for and didn't.
