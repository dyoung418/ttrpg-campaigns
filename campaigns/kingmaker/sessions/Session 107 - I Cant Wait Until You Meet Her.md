# Session 107 — I Can't Wait Until You Meet Her

> **Date**: 2026-08-03
> **Status**: Complete — **ended at the start of a new fight**
> **Previous Session**: [[Session 106 - The Thing He Wears]]

---

## What was established *(GM rulings — canon)*

**[[The Wriggling Man]] spoke — and he said [[Nyrissa]] brought them here.** His first line in the
campaign, delivered with his eyeless face turning to take in each character in turn:

> *"Well, aren't you a motley crew? **Narissa was right to bring you here.** I can't wait until you meet her."*

This is the session's biggest ruling and it reframes the whole march. The party has spent since
[[Session 100 - She Dropped Your Letter|Session 100]] believing they **chased** Nyrissa into
[[Thousandbreaths]] — that taking [[Lyriel]] was a mistake that gave them the trail. He says the
opposite: **the abduction was the invitation, and they took it.** They are not intruders. They are
expected guests, and something about their arrival is *useful*.

**He escaped.** He did not fight to the death. When [[Lucas]] cast a 6th-level *divine wrath* at the
edge of the wall of force, his **contingency** fired and he **translocated out of sight mid-fight** —
by his statblock, *dimension door* to **M6, [[House at the Edge of Time|Nyrissa's Garden]]**, triggered
at ≤50 HP. The party never saw where he went. The fight ended with an empty battlefield and no body.
He has **fast healing 20** and the party correctly guessed he is regenerating right now. *(The second
phase of this fight is owed, not cancelled — see [[The Wriggling Man]].)*

**The trophy cannot be pickpocketed.** [[Pavel]] tried to lift it off him with a Steal action.
Danny described **the jewelry sinking down into his chest as the worms swarmed over it.** The swarm
*protects* it — it is not a loose object on a body, it is inside him. It left the room on him. Danny
also described the piece as a **locket**, which narrows the open "what is it" question in
[[Lyriel's Stolen Jewelry]].

**His weaknesses are now public knowledge.** [[Lucas]]'s **Whispers of Weakness** returned it clean:
**lowest save Fortitude; weak to area damage and splash damage.** The party spent the rest of the
night re-arming around that — fireballs, junk bombs, splash weapons — and they will come back to the
rematch built for it.

**Also established at the table:**

- **He resists grapple but not shove**, and **he is not immune to *immobilized*** — a real control
  handle the party found and will reuse.
- **He can occupy a PC's space** (Squirming Embrace), which forces flat checks on ranged attacks
  through the occupied square. He parked in [[Astor]]'s space repeatedly and used the party as cover.
- **[[Briar|Briara]] in chainsword form acts as a *vorpal* blade against denizens of the First
  World** — DC 37 Fortitude, ~50 damage on the strike. Established explicitly in play.
- **The party is level 18.**

**The party reached Level 2 of the House.** After the fight they regrouped on the second-floor
walkway, rested 10 minutes, and went through a door into a **huge ornate chamber** — vaulted ceiling,
supports carved as serpentine dragons, **seven statues of a beautiful woman** with a sword or in
aggressive poses, two long tables and benches around a large marble serpent-dragon. **Two of
[[Nyrissa]]'s pets** were hiding under the tables; the moment they were seen they flew out.
**Session ended on initiative.** The room is **L1, the Great Hall** *(GM-confirmed)*; the pets are
[[Barbtongued Wyverns]]. **The seven statues are Nyrissa — nobody has recognized her yet.**

## What actually happened *(actual play)*

> [!note] Shape of the night
> One very long, very tactical boss fight that the boss **walked away from**, a ten-minute rest, and
> ninety seconds of exploration before the next fight started. Four sessions in a row have now ended
> in or on the edge of combat.

### Housekeeping first

Play opened with the table re-establishing where everyone was. The explored map had **reset** — Danny
had moved the group to a *copy* of the scene while changing maps, which wiped shared fog of war — and
initiative order and [[Astor]]'s initiative score both needed correcting. [[Riven]] summarized the
board for everyone: they were upside down, in a throne-like area, fighting something *"composed of
words, not like a solid shape,"* that had hidden behind an illusion and flipped gravity. Then Pavel's
turn.

### The throne room, under Reverse Gravity (K17)

- **Pavel takes the long way down.** He rode the reverse-gravity field off the bridge, **up to the
  ceiling, across it, and back down to the floor** — landing beside the Wriggling Man with no fall
  damage. He drew **[[Briar|Briara]] in chainsword form** and connected: ~50 damage, and against
  *denizens of the First World* the weapon works as a **vorpal blade, DC 37 Fortitude**. The follow-up
  swing missed badly.
- **He talks.** *(See above — the ruling of the session.)* Then he spent his actions on **quickened
  casting** (1/day; it failed on Pavel) and **Suggestion** on Astor:
  > *"I can't believe you guys didn't check the courtyard. I mean, that's an ideal place to hide an
  > abductee. You should go check that right now."*

  **Astor resisted.** Both spells whiffed and his turn ended with nothing to show for it — but the
  *content* of the Suggestion is the tell. See **Threads opened**.
- **Astor answers in kind.** Astor taunted straight back: *"We've already looked through the courtyard
  and there's nothing here — but you seem like a better lead. So why don't you tell us where the
  prisoner is?"* He is the first PC to demand Lyriel's location out loud.
- **Lucas cracks the statblock open.** **Whispers of Weakness** → **lowest save Fortitude, weak to area
  and splash.** He followed with **Pernicious Poltergeist / telekinetic storm** as a sustained area
  effect, carefully placed to catch the worm without wrecking Pavel and Lee. Everyone saved, including
  the target — but the information was the real prize.
- **The rest of the round.** [[Riven]] fired **enchanting shot** from the ceiling, standing upside
  down. **Astor** improvised **Quick Alchemy** acid, missed, splashed his own allies (both had acid
  resistance), then **shoved** the worm for bludgeoning — the table having worked out it **resists
  grapple but not shove**. [[Milo]] asked what happens if he leaves the gravity zone, was told
  *40 feet, 20 damage*, and had **Rindle** ferry him down in stages instead; he drew the **Mind
  Renderer's Baton** and held it. [[Lee]] feinted for a bleeding finisher and **critically missed** —
  then burned a **hero point** and missed again.

### Silence, tendrils, and the grind

- **Pavel silences him.** A **scroll of *Silence*** out of the **Sleeves of Storage** (the table spent
  a minute on how hokey that item name sounds out loud) landed on the Wriggling Man. He answered by
  lashing Astor with tendrils and then **stepping into Astor's space** — pressed bodily into him, so
  every ranged attack at the worm risked hitting Astor.
- **The wheel turns and nothing lands.** Lucas sustained his area spell; the worm kept saving. Riven
  rolled flat checks to shoot past Astor, landed **homing shot** and **Frostbite**. Astor's second
  shove **critically failed** — prone, then **Kip Up** — and he kept taunting while bleeding. Lee's
  finisher **crit-missed again** and cost him his panache. Milo and Rindle held the ranged line with
  **Lock On** and the baton. Riven's later volleys finally left the creature **bloodied but standing**,
  and the table said out loud what everyone was thinking: *really hard to kill.*
- **Pavel goes quiet.** He tried acid, tried wind, then discovered he could keep casting **through his
  own *Silence*** using **subtle casting**, and started hiding instead — **stealth against tremorsense**
  became his whole game for the rest of the fight. It worked: he got **undetected** more than once, and
  the worm shifted its attention to Astor and Lee whenever it lost him.

### The wall

- **Lucas splits the room.** A **Wand of Wall of Force** and a 20-foot wall, placed to cut the
  Wriggling Man off from the party. The table then spent real time on geometry — what shoots over,
  what spells reach, who can cross.
- **The party adapts around it.** **Milo** lobbed a **Necklace of Fireballs** over the top, placed to
  land only on the enemy side, and later tried a **junk bomb** — both leaning hard on the freshly
  discovered splash weakness. He also used **Clockwork Celerity** to reposition Rindle, and the table
  confirmed the worm is **not immune to immobilized**. **Riven** kept firing over the wall and missed a
  lot; the swarm body is genuinely hard to hit with arrows. **Astor** switched to medic, working
  **Battle Medicine** and **Treat Wounds** on Lee's persistent damage, then **Cloud Jumped clean over
  the wall** to rejoin Pavel and shove the worm again. **Lucas** flew higher and dropped a **6th-level
  *Heal*** on Astor. **Lee** looked at the wall, said he's an acrobatics man not an athletics man, and
  **readied an action** instead of forcing a bad jump.
- **Pavel goes for the locket.** With the worm in reach he tried a **Steal** — and Danny described the
  **jewelry sinking into its chest as the worms swarmed around it.** Not a failed roll so much as a
  closed door. He hid again.

### He leaves

- **Lucas came to the edge of the wall and cast a 6th-level *Divine Wrath*.** The spell **triggered a
  reaction**: his **contingency** fired and he **vanished instantly.** No death, no body, no line on
  the way out.
- **The wall dropped on an empty room.** The party searched the nearby doors, worked out he had almost
  certainly **regenerated while out of sight**, and debated chasing him versus resting. They talked
  about how far he could have gone — a mile, a teleport, anywhere — and concluded pursuit was a
  coin-flip. **They chose to spend 10 minutes.** Lucas ran group healing (costing a little damage to
  give a lot back) and Astor burned a **mutagen** for extra healing before it expired. **Riven tied off
  a rope** for the group, then **listened at the doors** and reported hearing something in one of the
  rooms.

### The ornate chamber

The party went in. Vaulted ceiling; supports carved as **serpentine dragons**; **seven statues of a
beautiful woman**, some holding a sword, some in aggressive poses; two long tables with benches
arranged around a **large marble serpent-dragon**. The room revealed fully — and with it, **two of
Nyrissa's pets crouched beneath the tables.** The instant they were seen, they **flew out to attack.**

Danny called it there, noting this one should be short compared to the Wriggling Man.

## Threads opened

- **"Narissa was right to bring you here."** The single most important sentence of the session. If it
  is true, the party's entire campaign — the kingdom, the blooms, the march on Thousandbreaths — is
  something Nyrissa *wanted*, and Lyriel was the hook, not the prize. It also fits: this is a queen who
  has spent centuries growing kingdoms in order to harvest them. **Decide before 108 whether he is
  telling the truth**, and whether *he* believes it. Nobody in the party has repeated the line back to
  each other yet.
- **He wants them in the courtyard.** His *Suggestion* tried to send Astor back to **K2, the Great
  Courtyard the party skipped in [[Session 105 - The Thing That Cannot Be Forgiven|105]]** — where the
  **Elite Sard is still alive and unfought**. Whether that is a genuine attempt to feed them to the
  guardian, a bid to buy himself time, or a real hint about a real hiding place, it is the first
  concrete thing he has ever *wanted* from them.
- **Astor was bluffing** *(confirmed by the GM)*. The party has **not** searched K2 — in 105 they crossed
  only its edge and went in by a side door. Astor lied to the Wriggling Man's face to deny him the
  satisfaction, which means **both sides of that exchange were lies** and neither knows it. The bait is
  therefore still fully loaded: the party genuinely doesn't know what's in the courtyard, and the
  **Elite Sard** is still sitting in it.
- **He is out there healing.** The party knows it. He knows they know. The rematch is on his clock, and
  he chooses when and where — see [[The Wriggling Man]] for how phase two should differ from phase one.
- **The locket is *inside* him.** It cannot be stolen, cut off, or looted from a corpse without going
  through the swarm. That raises the cost of recovering it and it means **killing him is now the only
  way to get it back** — which pulls against making him talk, exactly the vise the encounter design
  wanted.
- **Riven's parents never came up.** The recognition beat did not fire. See the note under **Next
  session setup** — the window did *not* close.

## Threads closed / advanced

- **The Wriggling Man has a voice, a personality, and a stated agenda.** Courteous, delighted,
  unhurried — exactly the register the encounter design called for. He is no longer an ambush and a
  spell effect.
- **The party has solved his statblock.** Fortitude is the soft save; area and splash are the answer;
  shove works, grapple doesn't; immobilized sticks. This fight is now a **problem they know how to
  solve**, which is why phase two has to change the terms rather than repeat them.
- **Phase one of the Trophy design is spent.** He talked and he escaped without ever bargaining
  Lyriel's location — the *"her location in pieces, for concessions"* half of the design is **still
  unspent** and is now the obvious opening move for the rematch.
- **The throne was never contested.** Nobody made a run at the dais and the portal to M7c was never
  opened. The door is still shut and the doorkeeper is still alive.
