## What was established *(GM rulings — canon)*

**The party finally met [[The Wriggling Man]].** After a run-up seeded since
[[Session 100 - She Dropped Your Letter|Session 100]] — the abduction at the gate, Linzi's Sending, the
freed guards' whisper that *"the one with many faces keeps the elf-woman"* — the party reached the Throne
Room (K17) and the House's arch-deceiver is finally on-screen.

**[[Lyriel]] is not with him.** This is the session's structural ruling and it overturns a lot of prep. She
was never hidden in the throne room. She has been moved deeper into the House and is held **with
[[Nyrissa]] herself** in the moonlit plaza (**M7c**) — the Fable reached *through the throne he is standing
on*. He is the doorkeeper, not the jailer.

**He wears a piece of her jewelry as a trophy, and [[Riven]] saw it.** Not concealed, not a discovered
reveal — worn openly. It confirms he is the fey from the [[Nightmarch]] gate, it proves she was real and in
his hands, and it announces that she is *not in this room*. See [[Lyriel's Stolen Jewelry]]. *(The specific
piece is still an open GM decision — options are laid out in that note.)*

**The session ended mid-combat.** The fight is live; **107 resumes in initiative.** This is the third
session in a row to end mid-fight ([[Session 103 - The Shape of Where I Used to Be|103]] on the Rimebridge,
[[Session 104 - The First Mercy|104]] in the guardhouse) — worth noting as a pacing pattern, not
necessarily a problem.

## The endgame spine — **revised in 106**

> [!important] What changed
> The spine carried from [[Session 104 - The First Mercy|104]]/[[Session 105 - The Thing That Cannot Be Forgiven|105]]
> had **five** steps, with Lyriel's rescue as a separate mid-crawl climax that *taught* the party the mercy
> thesis in time to apply it to Nyrissa. With Lyriel moved into M7c, **steps 3–5 collapse into one
> confrontation.**

1. **The Gatehouse (104–105)** — the ashamed dead. *Some "monsters" want release, not death.* The verb
   **forgive** is learned and practiced. ✅ done
2. **The courtyard Sard (105)** — the counter-example in miniature. A made guardian with no Shame; mercy
   does nothing. *Some enemies you just fight.* ✅ done
3. **[[The Wriggling Man]] (106 — in progress)** — the full counter-example, and now also **the door.** No
   Shame, no hostage, no honest reason to spare him: the purest statement of *some enemies you simply
   kill.* He guards the throne, which is the portal to M7c.
4. **M7c — the Queen and the healer, together (the finale).** [[Lyriel]] and [[Nyrissa]] in the same room.
   The rescue and the choice are now **one scene**, not two.

**The risk this creates, and the opportunity:** the old structure let Lyriel *say the thesis* — *"Some
hurts can't be cut out. You can only forgive them"* — safely, before the final choice, so the party could
carry it in. Now she says it **in front of Nyrissa, under threat, with Riven's hands full.** That is
higher-stakes and better theatre, but it is also easy to lose in the noise of a boss fight. **It needs
deliberate room.** Options for how to protect the beat are in [[Lyriel]]'s GM notes and
[[House at the Edge of Time]] (M7c).
