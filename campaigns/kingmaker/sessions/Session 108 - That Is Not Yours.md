## Step 2 — Strong Start

> [!info] Read-Aloud
> The tables erupt — two long shapes come out from beneath them on membrane wings, barbed tongues
> already lashing — and seven carved women watch it happen with swords in their stone hands.
>
> And then the ceiling moves.
>
> Something that has been folded up against the vaulting since before you opened the door comes apart
> and falls. Not a body — a column of them, thousands of them, pouring down through forty feet of air
> and re-knitting itself on the way. It lands behind Pavel. It is whole. It is unhurt. It has been
> waiting.
>
> ***"That,"*** it says pleasantly, ***"is not yours."***

> [!note] That last line is the first true thing he has ever told them
> **[[Briar]] *is* [[Nyrissa]]'s** — her stolen capacity to love, forged out of her as a punishment.
> He is not lying, and the party has no way to know that. Let it sit.

**Why this opening works:** it fixes both halves of the 107 slog at once. The **wyverns become his second
front**, so he needs no swarm shards, and **he is not standing in an empty room waiting to be shot.**

## Step 4 — Secrets and Clues

> [!secret] GM Only — keep abstract from discovery method

1. The throne in K17 is a door, and it opens for someone who sits in it and imagines holding court before a crowd of thousands.
2. [[Lyriel]] is alive, and she is being held in the room beyond that door — with [[Nyrissa]] herself.
3. Nyrissa watches every room in this House at once, and can kill any servant she is looking at with a single thought.
4. The seven statues in this hall are Nyrissa.
5. The Wriggling Man cannot destroy [[Briar]] — his queen needs the sword whole.
6. He has spent his escape. There is no second disappearance left in him.
7. The swarm cannot hold what it has taken when it is scattered.
8. He keeps things taken from the people he has hurt, and the locket is not the first.
9. His mortal life ended on a mission he failed, and he has never once said where.
10. Nyrissa has taken nine hundred and ninety-nine kingdoms, and [[Freehaven]] was always meant to be the thousandth.
11. One of you has been lying dead in this House for a very long time.

> [!tip] Which ones are load-bearing
> **#1 and #2 are the session's spine.** The party has **never been told** the throne is the portal, and
> still does not know where Lyriel is — 106 left it open and he never bargained it away in 107. Without
> these two, "reach the throne this session" cannot happen. Both are things he would gloat and both are
> things [[Briar|Briara]] knows. **Do not be precious about them.**
>
> **#7 is a mechanical lifeline** — it's how they learn that area/splash pushes back the Engulf track.
> Spend it if the sword is at Stage 2 and nobody has worked it out.
>
> **#9 is the demon-and-the-worm door, held open with one finger.** True regardless of what you decide;
> commits to nothing. See [[The Demon's Legacy]].
>
> **#10 recontextualizes *"Narissa was right to bring you here."*** If Freehaven was always the intended
> thousandth, he wasn't gloating about a trap — he was telling them what they're **for**.

## Step 5 — Fantastic Locations

### The Hall of Seven Swords — [[House at the Edge of Time|L1, the Great Hall]]
- **Seven Nyrissas, armed.** She is in this room seven times over, sword raised, and nobody has recognized her. [[Briar|Briara]] would, instantly.
- **The long tables and benches.** The wyverns' hiding place — now cover, high ground, and things to overturn. **This is [[Lee]]'s route to a flying enemy.**
- **The marble serpent-dragon.** Huge, climbable, breaks line of sight, drops whoever climbs it into the middle of everything.

### Four Beds Made for No One — [[House at the Edge of Time|L3, the Guest Rooms]]
- **Four large rooms, beds turned down, never slept in.** Kept ready for centuries for guests who never came.
- **Temporal echoes in the mirrors** — younger and older versions of the PCs, glimpsed and gone. *This is the only honest vector for [[Pavel's Parentage]] and [[Milo's Lost Brother]] if you ever want them.*
- **[[Lucas]]'s skeleton** *(see GM Notes)*.

### The Door They Walked Away From — [[House at the Edge of Time|K17, the Throne Room]]
- **The marble throne.** The portal to M7c, [[Nyrissa]], and [[Lyriel]]. They fought two sessions over this room and never touched it.
- **The ceiling murals** — the wolf with a severed hand, the viper at the crib, the bear stalking a mother and child. The mind-state of the realm: three pictures of a predator arriving at something defenceless, in the room where the party is about to open a door.
- **The wreckage of two sessions.** Scorch marks, the reverse-gravity ceiling, the rope [[Riven]] tied off. It should feel like returning to a place that beat them.

### Boss fight prep — apply before play

Full build in [[The Wriggling Man]] → *Session 108 — The Ambush in the Great Hall*. The short version:

- **Engulf the Blade** — 3-stage track (Grip → Sheathed → Swallowed), **Acrobatics +39**, three successes needed. **Area or splash damage pushes it back a stage.** Hard cap ~4 rounds.
- **Reform the Throat** (1 action) — ends a *silence* on him. Pavel's counter still works, it just costs him a third of a turn instead of the fight. *"You did that to me once."*
- **No third escape.** Contingency spent, not re-prepared. He has *dimension door* only to **leave with the sword.**
- **He cannot destroy Briar** — Nyrissa needs it whole.

**The number fixes that stop this being another slog:**

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| Physical resistance **20 → 10** | Three of six PCs had their damage gutted in 107 |
| Precision immunity → **Briara's vorpal clause cuts through it** | Gives [[Pavel]] his signature move back; thematically earned |
| Fast healing 20 → **only if he took no damage since his last turn** | Rewards pressure instead of punishing insufficient burst |
| **Keep HP at 325 — do NOT use the +420 mod** | Mark coils at 225 / 150 / 75; each bursts visibly and costs him something named |
| **Skip the swarm shards** | The wyverns already are his second front |

> [!danger] Difficulty — go in with your eyes open
> **200 XP** (PL+3 boss at 120, two PL+0 at 40 each) against a six-player band where **Severe is 180 and
> Extreme is 240**. And **they have not had a long rest** — ten minutes restores focus points and Treat
> Wounds, **not spell slots**. [[Lucas]] spent a 6th-level *Heal*, a 6th-level *Divine Wrath*, *Void
> Warp*, and a sustained *telekinetic storm*. [[Pavel]] burned his *Silence* scroll. [[Milo]] spent a
> fireball bead and junk bombs.
>
> **Keep a dial ready:** one wyvern disengages, arrives a round late, or breaks off to guard the door.

## GM Notes

> [!warning] GM Only

### The skeleton in the guest room *(L3)*

**It is [[Lucas]].** Old, dry, settled in dust — not fresh. The horror is not *"you will die,"* it is
**"you have already been dead here a long time."**

- **What identifies him:** his medallions. His backstory already says *"he wears many medallions, at
  least one of which seems to move of its own volition."* The skeleton wears them. **And one of them is
  still moving.** Fox-skulled, so nobody has to guess.
- **The proof it's the future:** it holds **[[Great Aunt Sumi]]'s healer's kit** — an object Lucas has
  never owned. He takes it off his own bones. That *is* the item acquisition (Step 8). From then on he
  carries something whose entire known history is *"found on Lucas's body."*
- **The beat that lands:** Lucas has never in his life been alone — the ancestors are a constant,
  meddling, sprawling family reunion in his head. Standing over his own body, **nothing answers.** The
  silence is the horror, not the bones.
- **The crueller variant:** *[[Great Aunt Sumi]] is there, waiting, and she is not surprised.* Use only
  if you want to hurt Jon. It is very good.
- **Everyone sees it.** The party identifies it together, so Lucas doesn't get to keep it private. Him
  being watched while he looks at it is the scene.
- **It stays.** Physical, permanent, a landmark they can return to, take from, or bury. Much worse than
  a vision.

> [!important] Never adjudicate whether it is *a* future or *the* future
> Nobody in the House has worked out whether it shows echoes or forewarnings — that is already an
> established open question the party has failed to solve. But the precedent is grim: [[Astor]]'s gouge
> and Lucas's own chalk mark **both came true within minutes.** Let Lucas decide what it means and never
> confirm it. A prophecy you refuse to rule on stays frightening; one you rule on becomes a countdown or
> a shrug.

### Branching the aftermath — decide the shape, improvise the details

- **He dies.** [[Lyriel's Stolen Jewelry|The locket]] comes out of the wreckage. [[Riven]]'s beat. Quiet.
- **He escapes with [[Briar|Briara]].** She screams and **does not stop** — she gets louder the closer
  she is to [[Nyrissa]] (*"I can feel the shape of where I used to be"*), so **the thing he stole is the
  thing that gives him away.** He can't lurk; he runs straight to M7c. [[Pavel]] hears her best. He also
  gloats the destination on the way out — *"She'll want to see this. You know where she is; you've been
  standing on the door all night."* **A loss becomes a chase with a countdown, not a wall.**
- **He breaks off empty-handed.** Worst outcome for pacing. If it happens, give him a reason (he got what
  he came to learn) rather than letting it fizzle.

### Standing reminders

- **[[Astor]] was bluffing** about having searched the courtyard. The party has never been in K2 and the
  **Elite Sard is still alive** — the GM is **not** steering toward it, and the party wasn't tempted.
- **The demon-and-the-worm merge is on hold, gated on this fight being good.** If it lands, the option
  is live again *this session*. Cheapest way to keep the door open: **one line on the drop** — his eyes
  catch on **[[Feywind]]** in Riven's hands. *"Where did you get that bow?"* Say it or don't.
  *(Caveat: this configuration puts him on Pavel and Riven on wyvern duty, so the beat is less available
  than in a straight duel.)*
- **Still unclaimed in the House:** the K14 healing pool, the six jade candlesticks in K11, the live
  Eldritch Echoes hazard in K13, and the two unidentified items the party is carrying — the
  [[Maestro's Flute]] and the [[Brass Telescope]].
