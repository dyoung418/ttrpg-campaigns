# Armag Reborn

> **Role**: Legendary barbarian resurrected; defeated at the vault of the old Armag's sword (Session 65, after the [[Fort Drevlev]] arc)
> **Status**: Defeated

## Background

A legendary barbarian figure who was reborn and wielded [[Ovinrbaane]], a legendary weapon. The [[Tiger Lords]] conspired with [[Hannis Drevlev]] against [[Fort Drevlev]]; the party then tracked them to the vault of the old Armag's sword, where Armag Reborn had already claimed Ovinrbaane and slain his own henchmen. The party defeated him there (Session 65) and rescued the kidnapped daughter. [[Benedin]] was seriously wounded by Ovinrbaane in that fight.

Referenced by [[Riven]] as one of the party's major accomplishments: *"you freed Drevlev and stopped Armag Reborn."*
