# Barbtongued Wyverns

**Creature 18** (×2) | Dragon | *Nyrissa's pets*

The two barbtongued wyverns [[Nyrissa]] keeps in the **Great Hall (L1)** of the
[[House at the Edge of Time]] — the closest thing the Heartless Queen has to something she is fond of.
Per [[Chapter 10 - Sound of a Thousand Screams|Chapter 10]] they **fight to the death** and will not be
reasoned with; they have **no Shame entry**, so **Forgive Foe does not work on them.**

## Current Status

**Engaged — combat is live.** The party walked into the Great Hall at the end of
[[Session 107 - I Cant Wait Until You Meet Her|Session 107]] and found them **hiding beneath the long
tables.** The instant they were spotted they **flew out to attack.** The session ended on the first
round of initiative; **108 resumes here.**

Danny told the table this fight should be **short** compared to the [[The Wriggling Man|Wriggling Man]]
grind — which is the point of it. It is a palate cleanser between the boss that got away and whatever
comes next.

> [!success] Room confirmed by the GM — this is **L1, the Great Hall**
> As described at the table: vaulted ceiling, supports carved as **serpentine dragons**, **seven statues
> of a beautiful woman** (some with a sword, some in aggressive poses), and two long tables with benches
> around a **large marble serpent-dragon**.

## The Room — the Great Hall (L1)

Three things worth playing, beyond the wyverns themselves:

1. **The seven statues are [[Nyrissa]].** Seven versions of the same beautiful woman, armed or
   mid-lunge — a hall full of the Queen looking at herself with a sword in her hand. The party has now
   seen her face seven times before ever meeting her. Nobody has recognized her yet; [[Briar|Briara]]
   would, instantly, and it would cost her something to say so.
2. **The tables and benches are cover and terrain.** The wyverns were *under* them; they are a flying
   pair in a room full of things to break line of sight with, tip over, and fight around. Let the
   party use them too.
3. **It is a hall for feasting, and nobody has feasted here in centuries.** Same note the whole House
   keeps playing — a room built for a court that no longer exists.

## Tactics

Flying skirmishers. They burst from concealment, take the air under the vaulted ceiling, and use their
mobility against a party that spent all of 107 pinned in a reverse-gravity field. Their barbed tongues
and stings make them melee threats that do not want to *stay* in melee.

**Against this party:** [[Riven]] and [[Milo]]/[[Rindle (Construct)|Rindle]] are the natural answer —
this is an archer's fight after a night that punished archery. [[Lucas]] still has fly up. [[Lee]] and
[[Astor]] will want the wyverns brought down to them; grounding one is a real objective.

> [!secret] GM Only
> - **Keep it short, as promised.** Two Creature 18s against six level-18 PCs is a fair-to-easy fight
>   and it is *supposed* to be. Resist the urge to add adds. The party earned a win they can finish.
> - **They are guilt-free.** No Shame, no mercy option, nothing to forgive — same clean permission the
>   [[Ankou Assassins]] gave. After the gatehouse taught forgiveness, the House keeps offering
>   counter-examples, and that's the pattern working.
> - **They are also *pets*.** If [[Nyrissa]] is watching from M7c — and she is — she watches these two
>   die. That is a small, cheap, genuine loss for a woman who cannot feel losses. Consider whether she
>   reacts, and whether anyone can tell.
