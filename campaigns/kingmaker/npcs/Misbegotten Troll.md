# Misbegotten Troll

**Creature 18** | Guardian and anchor of [[Glade I — The Baleful Lantern]] | **Dead** — killed in [[Session 101 - The Heads Still Dream|Session 101]] when [[Milo]] sent [[Rindle (Construct)|Rindle]] into the Lantern to detonate

A regenerating fey-corrupted troll that holds the Baleful Lantern in [[Thousandbreaths]], commanding a pack of eight Black Smilodons. When bloodied, it retreats into the beehive tower to heal — then returns whole.

## Role in the AP

Serves as the normal tether of the Baleful Lantern glade — a beehive-shaped structure lit by decapitated human heads used as lanterns. During [[Chapter 10 - Sound of a Thousand Screams|Chapter 10]] the anchor transfers to the building itself, which the party must collapse to break the glade. *(In play the glade released the party when the troll died — see [[Session 101 - The Heads Still Dream|Session 101]].)* Also listed as a bloom encounter in [[The Month of Destruction]].

## Tactics

- **AP default:** when attacked from over 100 feet away, retreats into the Lantern to regroup; directs the Smilodons to attack archers in the back first.

- **Returns fully healed** after withdrawing into the Lantern to regenerate.
- **Burrowing emergence** (GM table ruling, Session 101): rather than crossing open ground into the party's ranged fire, it tunnels through the glade's corrupted earth (**Burrow Speed 30**) and erupts beneath the back line. Its emergence Strike grants a free Trip or Grab on a hit; adjacent creatures make a DC 36 Reflex save or fall prone. Telegraphed a round early (trembling ground, sifting dust).
- Commands the **8× Black Smilodons** (CR 14) — corrupted First World echoes of [[Cat]].
