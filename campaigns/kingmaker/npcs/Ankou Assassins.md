# Ankou Assassins

> **Creature 17** · 4× · Fey killers of the First World, hidden inside the wall paintings of the **Lower
> Hall (K4)** of the [[House at the Edge of Time]]. They serve [[Nyrissa]] — and they **also secretly spy
> for [[The Lantern King]]**, which the party does not know.

## Status

**Destroyed.** Fought across the end of
[[Session 105 - The Thing That Cannot Be Forgiven|Session 105]] and the first long stretch of
[[Session 106 - The Thing He Wears|Session 106]]. All of them and all their doubles are dead — two of them
**killed by their own spell** (see *Massacre*, below).

## What They Are

Ankou are First World nightmares — shadow-killers that serve powerful fey. [[Pavel]] recognized the type
on sight from prior experience (the party killed [[The Oaktop Ankou]], a thirty-foot Ankou general, during
the Oaktop campaign), and the table established that these things answer to **powerful fey or the eldest
beings of the First World.** That read is correct and incomplete: they report to Nyrissa, and they report
on Nyrissa to the Lantern King.

Unlike the [[Ghostly Guards]], **there is nothing here to forgive.** No Shame entry, no grief, no binding —
they are simply assassins. They ended up delivering the campaign's "some enemies you just fight" lesson
that the courtyard **Elite Sard** was designed to teach and never got to, because the party skipped the
courtyard.

## What The Party Learned On-Screen

- **Weak to cold iron.** [[Lucas]]'s **Whispers of Weakness** in 105 returned it plainly — and the claws
  themselves *"look like cold iron."* Lucas ran **Needle Darts with cold iron** for the rest of the fight.
- **Lowest save: Fortitude.** Same check.
- **Shadow doubles must be sustained.** The doubles they create only persist while the source caster spends
  an action sustaining them. When a caster died — or simply didn't sustain — its copies **winked out**. The
  party worked this out mid-fight and then **deliberately spread out to deny landing space** for new ones.
- **The line attack kills outright.** Their signature area attack dealt **104 void damage on a failure plus
  34 on the return**, and [[Astor]] flagged that anything it reduced to 0 HP **died instantly** rather than
  going down dying.
- **Massacre rebounds.** If **Massacre** fails to kill even one creature in the line, the void energy
  **surges backward** into the caster and everyone in the line. **Two Ankou casters killed themselves with
  it** in 106, each death spawning one final shadow double.

## How The Fight Went

**Session 105 — the reveal.** The party entered a lower hall of polished marble under a carving of **the
sun being devoured by a great dragon**, its walls painted with a **twilight forest full of dark shapes
among the trees.** Pavel read that something was wrong with the paintings and warned everyone back. Rather
than walk into it, **[[Riven]] shot the painting** — and the darkness peeled off the painted scene into a
living thing. The first exchange was brutal: the creature flew over the party, worked with doubles, and
opened with the line attack. Lucas answered with a 6th-rank **Holy Light** for 43 fire and the Whispers of
Weakness read; Astor **grappled the caster** to deny another line; [[Milo]] and [[Rindle (Construct)|Rindle]]
ran Overdrive, Lock On, and hammer work. Session ended with the fight live.

**Session 106 — the grind.** Most of a session. The party spent it separating real Ankou from duplicates
through a chokepoint doorway. Highlights: Pavel swapping [[Briar]] to **elven curve blade** for reach;
**Prismatic Spray** catching an Ankou's own double through the door; Riven's **Stifling Stillness** turning
the room to difficult terrain and forcing them to spend actions *breathing*; **Astor shoving one double
into a wall and another into a chandelier**, killing both; and the two Massacre suicides. [[Lee]] finished
the last one after tumbling through, and the final double spiralled out of existence with no one left to
sustain it.

## GM Notes

> [!secret] GM Only — the spy thread is unspent
> These four **also worked for [[The Lantern King]]**, watching Nyrissa's house from inside her own walls.
> The party killed them without ever learning that. Options:
> - Let it stay buried — the Lantern King simply loses eyes in the House at the worst moment for Nyrissa.
> - Surface it later: something in M4 (Nyrissa's library) or from the [[The Wriggling Man|Wriggling Man]]'s
>   mouth reveals that the Queen was being watched too, which reframes her as a prisoner of the Eldest
>   rather than their peer — useful ammunition for the **forgiveness** ending.
>
> The second option is the one that pays. It costs nothing now and it makes the endgame choice harder.
