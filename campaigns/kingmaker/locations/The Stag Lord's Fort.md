# The Stag Lord's Fort

> **Type**: Bandit stronghold (former)
> **Status**: Still standing — it became **Nightmarch's Old Fortress**, the seat of [[Freehaven]]'s government and the hall where the Great Feast was held ([[RP 14 — The Great Feast of Freehaven|RP14]])
> **Relevance**: Where the PCs defeated [[The Stag Lord]]; the [[Stag Lord's Helm]] was taken here

After the [[The Stag Lord|Stag Lord]] fell, the party made the fort their base and founded [[Nightmarch]] around it. The old walls were kept and built onto rather than torn down; the courtyard of the Old Fortress is where [[Edina Aldori]] said her piece to [[Pavel]] before the last bloom ([[RP 25 — Before the Latest Bloom - Edina Shoots her Shot|RP25]]).
